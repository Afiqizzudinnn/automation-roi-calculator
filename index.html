How it works

The maths, in plain words

  • Tasks per month = how often × people. Per day uses your working days; per week uses 4.33 weeks a month.
  • Hours saved = (minutes today − minutes after automation) × tasks per month ÷ 60.
  • Hourly cost = monthly salary ÷ (working days × 8 hours).
  • Money saved = hours saved × hourly cost. This counts salary only, with no overheads, so the real saving is usually higher.
  • Full-time staff freed up = hours saved per month ÷ one person's working hours per month.
  • Payback period = build cost ÷ money saved per month.
